Founded in Houston and built on the backbone of the Gulf Coast freight industry, Sunburst Truck Lines has been moving containers, cargo, and commerce for over three decades. We specialize in intermodal freight — the critical link between port, rail, and final destination.

Our fleet serves the Port of Houston, Port of Freeport, and major rail terminals, connecting Texas businesses to national and international supply chains with the reliability that only experience provides.

Based at 5121A Oates Rd, Houston TX 77013 — right where the work gets done.
