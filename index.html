Based in Cypress, TX and built on honest hard work, Virdi Transport runs a tight 19-truck operation serving Houston, Dallas, and the Gulf Coast. We specialize in flatbed, dry van, and heavy haul freight — the lanes that keep Texas moving.

Our drivers run dedicated lanes with 24/7 dispatch support, giving shippers the reliability and communication that big carriers promise but rarely deliver.

Based at 13911 Kluge Rd, Cypress TX 77429 — right where the work gets done.
